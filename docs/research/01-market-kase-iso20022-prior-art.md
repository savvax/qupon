## 2. Kazakhstan bond market mechanics

**Legal basis.** Law "О рынке ценных бумаг" No. 461-II of 2 Jul 2003 (edition as of 1 Jul 2026). Articles: **18-2** Общее собрание держателей облигаций; **18-4** Выкуп размещенных негосударственных облигаций; **19** Представитель держателей облигаций; **20** его функции; **31** Выплата дохода по ценным бумагам. Платежный агент. Погашение облигаций (https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/1041258).

**Record date & payment flow (Art. 31).** Coupon/principal is paid to holders registered in the CSD system **"по состоянию на начало последнего дня периода, за который осуществляются выплаты"** (start of the last day of the coupon period, CSD local time). Non-bank issuers must route money through the CSD's account; banks with a transfer licence may pay directly but must give the CSD the holder list and amounts; late payment accrues a daily penalty based on the NBK base rate (https://prg.kz/m/amp/document/1041258/7/370000).

**CSD paying-agent mechanics** (Rules of 12 Aug 2022): issuer transfers funds **≥2 business days before** period end; CSD builds the holder list as of the requested date/time (within 8 working hours); CSD pays holders / nominee accounts **within 2 business days** after receipt; sends issuer a report within 10 working days; unpaid amounts → unclaimed funds (https://backend.kcsd.kz/sk/document/740/ru/rules_payment_agent_NEW). Municipal-bond rules: holder positions struck **09:00 Almaty** on payment day (https://backend.kcsd.kz/sk/document/165/ru/rules_municipal_bonds5_new). Prospectus windows vary: e.g., Bank CenterCredit 7th issue pays within **20 calendar days** after period end; face 100 KZT; 9.0%; semi-annual; **30/360**.

**Chain:** Issuer → CSD (АО «Центральный депозитарий ценных бумаг», kcsd.kz) → brokers/custodians (nominee holders) → investors. CCP for KASE stock market = KASE Clearing Centre (KACC) since 25 Sep 2023 (https://kacc.kase.kz/en/about). Corporate bonds settle **T+2** (https://kase.kz/en/markets/corporate-bonds).

**Identifiers & conventions.** Trade code = 4-letter issuer code + lowercase suffix: **b** bond, **e** eurobond, **p** preferred share; **pp** = private placement board. ISIN prefixes observed: **KZ2C** (JSC issuers), **KZ2P** (LLP/MFO issuers), **KZMJ** (municipal), XS/US for eurobonds — the 2C/2P rule is an observed pattern, **UNVERIFIED**. Typical face value **1,000 KZT**, semi-annual or quarterly coupon (monthly at MFOs), **30/360** dominant.

## 3. ISO 20022 / ISO 15022 corporate-actions standards

**Messages (latest versions, ISO archive Feb 2025):** seev.031 CorporateActionNotification V15 (CANO ≈ MT564); seev.033 Instruction V13 (CAIN ≈ MT565); seev.034 InstructionStatusAdvice V15 (CAIS ≈ MT567); seev.035 MovementPreliminaryAdvice V16 (CAPA); **seev.036 MovementConfirmation V16** (CACO ≈ MT566); seev.037 MovementReversalAdvice; seev.038 Narrative (≈ MT568); seev.039 CancellationAdvice; meetings seev.001–008 (https://www.iso20022.org/catalogue-messages/iso-20022-messages-archive?search=seev). seev.036 confirms posting of cash/securities, with CashMovementDetails (CreditDebitIndicator, PostingDate, PostingAmount, GrossAmount, NetAmount, InterestRateUsedForPayment, ValueDate).

**Event codes (CAEV):** INTR interest payment; REDM full redemption at final maturity; MCAL full call before maturity; PCAL partial redemption w/o pool factor; PRED partial redemption with pool-factor reduction; BPUT put; BIDS issuer repurchase offer; TEND third-party offer; EXOF exchange; CONV conversion; BMET bondholder meeting; XMET extraordinary meeting; CHAN change; DFLT default; DVCA cash dividend; DRIP reinvestment (https://www.iso20022.org/15022/uhb/mt566-4-field-22f.htm). **CAMV:** MAND, VOLU, CHOS.

**Dates/identifiers per SCoRE (ECB AMI-SeCo rulebook, Dec 2025):** record date = positions struck end of day; ex-date relevant for equities only; both **COAF** (official) and **CORP** (account-servicer ID) required; business-day conventions Following/Modified Following (https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/publications/pdf/ecb.amiseco202512_scorecarulebook.en.pdf).

**Minimal on-chain field set (seev.031 → seev.036):** event header: `CorpActnEvtId` (CORP), `OffclCorpActnEvtId` (COAF), `EvtTp`, `MndtryVlntryEvtTp`, `FinInstrmId.ISIN`, `AnncmntDt`, `RcrdDt`, `PmtDt`, `PrcgSts`, `Ccy`; INTR: `IntrstPrd`, `IntrstRateUsdForPmt`, `DayCntBsis`, `GrssAmt`, `NetAmt`, `WhldgTax`; REDM/MCAL: `RedemptionPrice`, final coupon flag, `MtrtyDt`/`CallDt`; CACO per holder: `SfkpgAcct`, `ConfdBal`, `CdtDbtInd`, `PstngDt`, `PstngAmt`, `ValDt`, tx reference; CHAN/BMET: `MtgDt`, resolution, vote tally, regulator registration date.

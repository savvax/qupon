- `existing` is now an ISIN→row map (status/daySecs/mint); `buildShowcasePlan` skips only when the on-chain series is active AND on the definition's clock — closed or other-clock (old 4 s showcase) definitions are re-planned fresh.
- `config/featured-series.txt` is rewritten with exactly the mints of matching definitions, so stale mints (old showcase) drop off the board.
- Unit tests cover the three skip-rule cases (closed→re-seed, active other clock→re-seed, active same clock→skip); no network, no devnet run (lead does that).
- Watch: after re-list, if both old and new series share an ISIN, mintOf takes the last row — new series are created later so they win, but a pathological ordering could pick the old mint.

# 1110 — landing scroll story (lead)
- "How it works": the five step numbers light up one after another as the section scrolls in (CSS `animation-timeline: view()`, per-step range offset via `--i`), and on desktop a gradient rail under the steps fills with the scroll.
- GPU-only (scale/background on tiny elements), static where scroll timelines are unsupported, off under reduced motion; locale-stability and landing e2e green.

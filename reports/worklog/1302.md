# 1302 — DvP client, `trade:*` CLI, devnet trade demo

- `pnpm codama` now also renders the Solana DvP client into `clients/ts/src/dvp/generated` from the vendored IDL
  (`generatedFolder`; both folders are in the CI drift check and the eslint ignores). Helpers in `src/lib/dvp.ts`
  (PDAs, verified record read, status, leg selection) with 15 unit tests.
- Commands in `src/commands/trade.ts`: `trade:create` (CreateDvp + admit_trade), `trade:fund` (verifies the on-chain
  record, tops the leg up to its exact amount), `trade:settle` (SettleDvp + release_trade), `trade:cancel [--force]`
  (CancelDvp + release_trade, or the permanent-delegate return), `trade:list`; `state:dump` prints `trades`. Each
  registrar command is one transaction, so success never leaves a trade admitted.
- Settle/cancel take the parties from the `TradeAdmission`, so they still work (as a plain release) after a party
  closed the trade with RejectDvp.
- Deviation from the plan text, same result: the demo is the `DEMO_TRADE=1` mode of `scripts/demo-full.ts`
  (`make demo-trade`), like `DEMO_SEED` / `DEMO_UI`, instead of a separate script — it reuses issuance, the coupon
  flow and the report writer. Plan 13 and docs/02 updated.
- Run on devnet against the redeployed program: `reports/demo-trade.md` (21 transactions — trade settled 65/10 →
  60/15, strike refused with TradesInFlight, cancel, snapshot 15/25/60, coupon paid). Also checked by hand on that
  series: `trade:list`, and `trade:cancel --force` returning 1 escrowed bond to the seller.
- To watch: after a forced cancel the DvP record stays open with an empty frozen bond escrow (a little registrar rent);
  the trade's expiry uses the wall clock (`--expiry-min`, default 60).

# 91301 — re-thaw a registered investor's recreated bond account

- Confirmed with a LiteSVM probe: an Active investor closes their empty bond ATA, anyone recreates it, and it comes
  back frozen (DefaultAccountState). `issue_units` then failed with Token-2022 0x11, `reinstate_investor` with
  `InvestorNotActive`, `suspend_investor` with Token-2022 0xd (freezing a frozen account) — locked out for good, and
  the registrar could not even suspend the investor.
- Fix in `handle_set_investor_status`: reinstate also accepts an Active investor whose bond ATA is frozen and thaws
  it; suspend skips the freeze CPI when the account is already frozen. No interface or error-code change.
- Tests in `tests/registry.rs`: `recreated_bond_account_is_thawed_by_reinstate`,
  `investor_with_a_recreated_bond_account_can_be_suspended`; reinstate on an Active investor with a thawed account
  still fails with `InvestorNotActive`.
- Done on the 1301 branch so the program is redeployed once.

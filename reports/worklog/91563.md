# 91563 — autopilot backs off on persistent chain refusals

- Scheduler: per (series, step) exponential backoff on chain refusals — 10 s doubling, cap 15 min; first refusal WARN, repeats DEBUG; reset on success or step change (injected-clock test pins 10/20 s).
- Ownership gate: Tick drives only series with a registry row, a sandbox row (series_settings.sandbox_id) or a featured showcase mint; unowned demo-clock series skipped, logged once per process. Store read failure falls back to driving everything (pre-91563 behaviour).
- TradesInFlight: on a sandbox series the scheduler lists admitted DvP trades (`trade:list` via new `executor.RunRaw`) and cancels them (`trade:cancel`); real series keep the operator rule.
- Tests: existing harness now registers the test series (ownership); new tests for backoff schedule, reset on success, skip rule, stale-trade release. `make verify` green. Watch: the sandbox trade cancel cancels *all* listed admitted trades of a sandbox series — acceptable for demo artifacts, revisit if live visitors' trades get cancelled mid-funding.

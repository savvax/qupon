  shared codes (CAEV/CAMV/A001–A005), minor-unit `Amount` with cash-mint
  decimals, Almaty dates, `Refs` → SplmtryData/AddtlInf.

  main.go builds one client for poller + index + ISO. `chain.Client` gained
  FetchAction/FetchEntitlement/ActionEntitlements (action key memcmp @9).

  for 031; structural + amount substrings for 036 since goldens use invalid
  base58 placeholders). xmllint --noout when installed, else Go parser.

  (same derivation register_investor uses) instead of fetching Investor;
  cash symbol mapped from the two devnet mints in config/devnet.json (tKZT
  default); pay_batch tx signature omitted (not stored on-chain) — slot,
  programId, cluster are the on-chain proof.

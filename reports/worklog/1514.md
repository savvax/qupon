# 1514 — holder-chosen payout route and bank payout confirmation (lead, Opus, during the Z.ai limit)
- `PayoutRoute ["route", investor]` set only by the holder's signature (registrar → Unauthorized); bank needs sha256 of the bank details, wallet a zero hash. The registrar cannot redirect payouts.
- `pay_batch` routes bank holders to the paying agent's cash ATA only (any other destination → AccountOwnerMismatch), marks `PaidToAgent`, counts `bank_pending`; `confirm_bank_payout` (paying agent) → Paid/Retired; `complete_action` needs no pending bank payouts. Redemptions burn at pay regardless of the route; `retire_batch` skips PaidToAgent (no double count).
- CLI `payout:route`, `payout:confirm-bank` (only hashes on-chain); `action:pay` reads each route (route byte at offset 41 — the account is not in the IDL because instructions take it unchecked) and creates the agent ATA idempotently. Go event layouts (26 events).

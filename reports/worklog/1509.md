# 1509 — spike: record date by transfer-hook gate — GO
- The program is the bond mint's transfer hook (`execute` with the SPL discriminator, Anchor 1.2 `#[instruction(discriminator = …)]`); validation account with 3 extra metas: series (seeded by the mint), checkpoint PDAs of the source and destination owners (seeded by bytes 32..64 of the token accounts).
- After `record_at` a transfer passes only if both owners' checkpoints cover the record; snapshot-then-transfer in one tx works (41k CU); a direct `execute` call is rejected via `TransferHookAccount.transferring`; a hooked transfer without extras fails in Token-2022.
- Solana DvP `SettleDvp` forwards our extras (`leg_a_extras_count`) and the gate holds inside it: settlement after `record_at` waits for the escrow owner's (SwapDvp PDA) and the buyer's snapshot; marks + settle in one tx = 73k CU. Plain hooked transfer = 30k CU.
- Series fields (hook, record_kind, record_seq, record_at) are carved from `reserved` (size stays 389 B); Checkpoint = 77 B. Non-hooked series behave exactly as before (all existing tests unchanged).
- `mark_snapshotted` is a spike stand-in; task 1510 folds the checkpoint into `snapshot_batch`, creates checkpoints at registration/admission, attributes escrow units to the seller, clears `record_at` at finalize and blocks minting while a record is pending.
